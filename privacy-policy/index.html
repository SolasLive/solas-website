Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 12, 2026

Welcome to My Solas. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application ("App") and website.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: When you create an account, we may collect information such as your name, email address, profile picture, and date of birth.
  • Audio and Voice Data: To provide our core feature of live voice rooms, we require microphone access (RECORD_AUDIO permission). Audio streams are transmitted in real-time. We do not permanently store your voice chats on our servers.
  • Foreground Services: We utilize Android Foreground Services for microphone and media playback. This allows your voice connection and audio playback to continue seamlessly even when the app is in the background.
  • Device and Usage Information: We may collect information about your device, IP address, and how you interact with our App to improve performance and user experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and maintain your My Solas account.
  • To enable live voice communication between users.
  • To personalize your experience and provide customer support.
  • To analyze usage patterns and improve our platform's functionality.

3. Permissions Explained

Our Android App specifically requests the following sensitive permissions. By using these features, you consent to this access:

  • Microphone: Essential for you to speak in live voice rooms.
  • Foreground Service (Microphone & Media): Required so that you can continue listening to and participating in voice rooms while using other apps or when your screen is locked.
  • Bluetooth: Used to ensure audio routing works correctly if you connect Bluetooth headsets.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with trusted third-party service providers strictly to operate our platform (for example, cloud hosting and database providers).

5. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information. You can request account deletion directly within the App or by visiting our Account Deletion Page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@solas-live.online.
