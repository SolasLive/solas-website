Safety & policy

Community Guidelines

Last Updated: August 12, 2026

At My Solas, we strive to create a vibrant, positive, and safe environment for all our users. To ensure a great experience, we ask everyone to follow these Community Guidelines when participating in voice rooms, sharing memories, and interacting in chats.

1. Respect and Kindness

Treat everyone with respect. Do not engage in hate speech, bullying, or harassment. We do not tolerate attacks based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, or disability.

2. No Nudity or Sexual Content

My Solas is not a place for sexually explicit content. Do not upload pornographic images, use sexually explicit language, or broadcast sexually explicit audio in voice rooms.

3. Child Safety

We have a zero-tolerance policy for child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or the sexualization of minors. Any accounts found violating this rule will be immediately terminated and reported to the appropriate legal authorities.

4. Violence and Harm

Do not promote, threaten, or glorify violence. This includes self-harm, terrorism, organized crime, or wishing physical harm upon others.

5. Spam and Scams

Do not use My Solas for deceptive practices. This includes spamming voice rooms, phishing for personal information, running unauthorized lotteries/gambling, or defrauding users of virtual gifts and currency.

6. Intellectual Property

Respect the copyrights and trademarks of others. Do not upload content or broadcast copyrighted material without permission.

7. Enforcement and Reporting

If you encounter content or behavior that violates these guidelines, please use the in-app Report feature. Our moderation team reviews reports and may take actions ranging from a warning to permanent account suspension.

Thank you for helping keep My Solas safe!
